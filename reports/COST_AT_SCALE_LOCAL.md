# RAG Cost at Scale — PUBLISH-BOTH Secondary (local mxbai-embed-large-v1)

<!-- style: v2, 2026-05-11 -->

Under markcrawl's shipping default embedder (`mixedbread-ai/mxbai-embed-large-v1`, local on CPU/MPS, $0 embedding cost), the v1.4 cost ordering is unchanged from the OpenAI primary: crawl4ai-raw cheapest at $3,781/yr, markcrawl 3rd at $4,748/yr. The $0 embedding saves only ~$7/yr at mid-scale because vector-DB hosting dominates the storage line; the real markcrawl reframe under mxbai is retrieval ranking (see [RETRIEVAL_COMPARISON_LOCAL.md](RETRIEVAL_COMPARISON_LOCAL.md) + the asymmetric-bias finding in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md)).

> **Single-trial measurement.** Each per-site number reported here is derived from one benchmark run's chunk counts. Network jitter, WAF state, and server load can shift per-site coverage between runs by single-digit percent, propagating into cost. Where CIs are reported, they reflect query-set sampling only — not run-to-run variance. Multi-trial validation is v1.5 work; see [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md#single-trial-measurement).
>
> **PUBLISH-BOTH secondary scope.** This report is a parallel never-mixed sibling of [COST_AT_SCALE.md](COST_AT_SCALE.md) (the OpenAI fairness-contract primary). Both leaderboards use the SAME chunk counts and SAME tool versions; the only varying input is the embedding price (OpenAI $0.02/1M tokens → local mxbai $0). Same per-site chunks-per-page measurements; identical methodology otherwise. Surfacing both lets a reader directly compare "what would I pay under the canonical fairness contract" vs "what would I pay under markcrawl's shipping default" with no other confounds.

**Generated by:** `python tools/cost_calculator.py --embedder=local-mxbai --sensitivity` against the v1.4 empirical chunks-per-page data (commit `b90831a`+).

## Mid-scale cost table (100K pages, 1K queries/day)

_Inputs: 100,000 pages, 1,000 queries/day, embedding **$0.00/1M tokens** (local mxbai), LLM input $3.00/1M tokens (Claude Sonnet), vector DB $0.10/1K vectors/month, 300 tokens/chunk._

| Tool | Storage/yr | Queries/yr | Total/yr | vs markcrawl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| crawl4ai-raw | $1,153 | $2,628 | $3,781 | −$967 (−20.4%) |
| crawl4ai | $1,190 | $2,628 | $3,818 | −$929 (−19.6%) |
| markcrawl | $1,463 | $3,285 | $4,748 | — |
| playwright | $2,282 | $5,256 | $7,538 | +$2,791 (+58.8%) |
| scrapy+md | $2,384 | $5,256 | $7,640 | +$2,893 (+60.9%) |
| crawlee | $2,734 | $6,242 | $8,975 | +$4,227 (+89.0%) |
| colly+md | $2,975 | $6,570 | $9,545 | +$4,797 (+101.0%) |

> **Why does markcrawl stay 3rd under local mxbai?** Vector-DB hosting (chunks/1000 × $0.10/month × 12) dominates the storage cost line. Embedding cost (chunks × tokens × $/1M) is ~$7/yr for markcrawl at this scale even at OpenAI prices — going to $0 with local mxbai saves only that $7. The cost ordering is driven almost entirely by per-tool chunks-per-page (markcrawl 12.19 vs crawl4ai-raw 9.61), not by the embedding-price line.
